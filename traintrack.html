Feature Deep Dive

Built for every RTO pain point.

Twelve features. Twelve problems eliminated. Each one built because an RTO manager told us it was costing them hours every week.

Pain: Manual AVETMISS errors

AVETMISS 8 Pre-Validation

Run a live scan before export. Flags every NAT file error — missing outcomes, invalid activity types, duplicate USIs — before you submit to NCVER. Supports SVTS, Smart & Skilled, Skills Assure.

Pain: Chasing USIs before census

Automated USI Verification

Every enrolment verified in real-time via the DET API. Students without a USI receive automated reminder sequences. Your register never contains an unverified USI again.

Pain: Trainer files out of date

Trainer Currency Tracker

Automated alerts fire 90, 60, and 30 days before currency lapses. Generates a Standards Clauses 1.13–1.16 evidence report on demand. No more audit surprises.

Pain: Hours marking written assessments

AI-Assisted Grading

AI surfaces suggested grades and key evidence points from student responses, benchmarked against your marking guide. Trainers review and confirm — they don't start from scratch.

Pain: Certificates issued weeks late

Auto Certificate + QR Codes

Certificates issue the moment a student achieves all unit outcomes. Every document contains a unique QR code linking to a live verification portal. Employers confirm authenticity in 5 seconds.

Pain: Scope drift from TGA registration

Live Training.gov.au Sync

Connects to the TGA API and syncs your registered scope daily. Attempting to enrol into an out-of-scope qualification triggers a compliance warning before the record is saved.

Pain: Paper enrolment forms

Branded Enrolment Engine

Embed a fully branded enrolment widget on your website. Collects all AVETMISS fields, LLND responses, USI consent, and payment in one flow. Lands directly in the SMS — zero re-entry.

Pain: Building ASQA audit packs under pressure

Audit Evidence Pack Generator

Select any Standards clause (1–8) and TrainTrack compiles the relevant records, trainer files, and outcomes into a structured evidence folder — exported as PDF or organised zip.

Pain: Attendance on paper rolls

Digital Attendance + Geo-Verify

Trainers mark attendance via the app — students self-sign via QR code on arrival. For workplace delivery, optional geo-location check-in verifies training occurred on-site.
